What We Believe

Four rules we don't bend on.

Principles that shape every commission, from a single shelf to a full kitchen fit-out.

01

Solid Timber, Always

No veneer, no MDF cores. If it looks like oak, it is oak, all the way through.

02

Joinery Over Fixings

Mortise and tenon, dovetail and housing joints wherever the design allows — glue and screws support, they don't carry.

03

One Team, Start to Finish

The person who draws your kitchen is the person who signs off on the install. Nothing is subcontracted out of sight.

04

Responsibly Sourced

Timber comes from mills we visit ourselves, with traceable, sustainably managed stock.

Milestones

Sixteen years, in brief.

2009

Founded on a single bench, taking on furniture repairs and small commissions nights and weekends.

2014

Moved into the current workshop and took on our first two apprentices.

2018

First full architectural fit-out — a library and staircase for a Georgian townhouse renovation.

2022

Reached our hundredth fitted kitchen and formalised our five-stage design process.

2026

Nine makers, one showroom, and a waitlist that now runs several months out.
