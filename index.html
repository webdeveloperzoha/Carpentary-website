03 — Our Process

From first sketch to final fit.

A fixed sequence, refined over sixteen years, so every client knows exactly what happens next.

01

Consultation & Brief

We visit the site, measure the space and talk through how you actually intend to use it — storage, seating, sightlines, the small habits that matter more than style boards.

02

Design & Drawings

Scaled drawings and material samples follow, refined with you across one or two rounds until every dimension and joint detail is signed off.

03

Material Selection

We source and hand-select boards for grain and colour, and bring you into the workshop to see and approve the timber before it's cut.

04

Handcrafted Build

Every piece is built on our own benches using traditional joinery, reinforced where it counts, with no shortcuts hidden behind a painted face frame.

05

Install & Finish

Our own fitters deliver and install on site, with final adjustments and finishing done in place so everything sits exactly as drawn.
